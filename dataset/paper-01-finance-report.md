# St Ambrose Hospice Care

## Board Paper: Finance Report to 30 June 2026

**Meeting: Board, 17 July 2026. Agenda item 5.**
**Author: Director of Finance. Purpose: to note.**

### Recommendation

That the Board notes the financial position at 30 June 2026 and the forecast outturn for 2026/27.

### Summary

The hospice is forecasting a deficit of £1.42m for 2026/27 against a budgeted deficit of £1.10m. Free reserves at 30 June stood at £4.61m, equivalent to 5.2 months of expenditure, against a reserves policy range of six to nine months. This is the second consecutive year in which the hospice has ended a quarter below its reserves policy floor.

### Income

Total income for the quarter was £3.94m against a budget of £4.21m, an adverse variance of £270,000.

Statutory income from the integrated care board was £1.18m, in line with budget. The contract renews on 31 March 2027 and no uplift has been offered in initial discussions. A separate paper on the renewal terms was presented to the Board on 15 July 2026 in response to the action raised in March.

Legacy income was £610,000 against a budget of £890,000. Legacy income is inherently lumpy and the finance team does not treat a single quarter as a trend, but the twelve month rolling total is now £2.71m against £3.34m for the equivalent period last year, a fall of 19 per cent. The Marchwood legacy, valued at approximately £340,000, remains in probate and is not recognised.

Retail income was £744,000 against a budget of £810,000. Four of the seventeen shops are loss making at contribution level. The independent review of the retail estate reported in March and its recommendation on those four shops comes to the Board in September.

Community fundraising and events were ahead of budget by £41,000, the only material favourable variance in the quarter.

### Expenditure

Total expenditure was £5.31m against a budget of £5.28m.

Pay costs were £3.86m, £94,000 above budget. The overspend is entirely in bank and agency nursing, which cost £412,000 in the quarter against a budget of £280,000. The action to produce an agency reduction plan was raised at the People Committee in December 2025, was due in April 2026, has been deferred once and remains outstanding. Substantive nursing vacancies stood at 11.4 whole time equivalents at 30 June, or 14 per cent of the establishment.

Non-pay costs were favourable by £61,000, largely through delayed spend on the electronic patient record procurement rather than through savings.

### Balance sheet and cash

Free reserves £4.61m. Designated funds £1.90m, of which £1.40m is designated for the inpatient unit roof replacement. Restricted funds £0.71m. Cash and short term deposits £3.22m. The hospice has no borrowings.

The roof replacement is fully designated but not yet funded from a cash flow perspective without drawing reserves below policy. A paper on funding options is due to the Finance and Audit Committee in October.

### Forecast and risk

The forecast deficit of £1.42m assumes legacy income recovers to budget in the second half, no further agency escalation, and the closure of the Ashcombe day therapy unit delivering £180,000 of in-year savings from September. If legacy income continues at the current run rate the deficit would be closer to £1.85m and free reserves would fall to approximately 4.1 months by 31 March 2027.

The three year financial recovery plan approved in January 2026 assumed a return to breakeven in 2028/29. On current performance that assumption no longer holds and the plan will need to be re-based. The Director of Finance proposes to bring a re-based plan to the November Board.

### Matters for the Board

The Board may wish to consider whether the reserves policy range remains appropriate given that the hospice has now operated below it for five consecutive quarters, and whether it wishes to see the re-based recovery plan earlier than November.
